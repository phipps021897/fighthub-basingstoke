About Fight Hub

Your Ultimate Martial Arts Destination

Step into a realm of strength, discipline and self-discovery at Fight Hub — Basingstoke's home for boxing, Muay Thai-style kickboxing, K1 and MMA.

Our Mission

A Gym for Every Fitness Level

Founded in 2023 by Marcus Cole, Fight Hub Basingstoke was built around one idea: martial arts training should be for everyone, not just competitive fighters. Our coaches run personalised, structured sessions across boxing, Muay Thai-influenced K1 kickboxing, MMA-style training and fitness, so whether you're here to get fit, build confidence or chase a fight career, you've got a programme that fits.

We keep classes small enough that coaches know your name, your goals and where you're at — beginner or advanced.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Personalised coaching across every class
  • Boxing, Muay Thai-style kickboxing & MMA under one roof
  • Dedicated kids and youth programmes
  • A modern, well-equipped training facility
  • A supportive, community-first gym culture

Our Coaches

Meet the Fight Hub Team

Every class at Fight Hub is led by experienced instructors who bring technical coaching and genuine care for every member's progress — from a nervous first class to fight night.

Marcus Cole

Owner

A fitness enthusiast and entrepreneur who established Fight Hub in 2023, bringing boxing and kickboxing expertise to build a community-driven fitness haven welcoming all levels.

Scott Morland

Coach

Training since age five in karate (2nd Dan black belt), with a background in amateur boxing and professional K-1 kickboxing, including the UCMMA Welterweight Title in 2018. Now coaches the next generation of fighters.

Taylor Baker

Coach

Over five years of martial arts training across MMA and boxing, including six months training in Thailand, and a blue belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Two years of coaching young people in confidence, discipline and self-defence.

Keaton Loader

Coach

A former national-level strongman (Basingstoke's Strongest Man), now specialising in boxing coaching. Has trained over 100 people in five years, with a focus on helping juniors grow into stronger, more confident individuals.

Deniss Kuzmins

Coach

Training in martial arts since 2012, competing professionally in K1 kickboxing (ranked 13th under 95kg by WKA UK) and holding double heavy and cruiserweight boxing champion titles. Works with individuals and groups on physical and mental development.
