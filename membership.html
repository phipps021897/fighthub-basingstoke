FAQs

Membership Questions

How do I book my free trial?

Use the "Book Free Trial" button above or head to our contact page and a member of the team will get you booked onto the right class.

Can I try both boxing and Muay Thai-style kickboxing?

Yes — your free trial can be used on any class, and many members mix boxing and K-1 Kickboxing once they've joined.

Do you offer family or sibling discounts?

Get in touch with the team to ask about options for families with multiple children training at Fight Hub.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Most memberships run on a 12-month agreement. Drop-in classes and off-peak gym sessions are available with no commitment at £10 per visit.
