Getting Started

New to Fight Hub?

Do I need any experience to join my first class?

No. Every class is coached for mixed-ability groups, and coaches break technique down from the fundamentals for anyone training for the first time.

Will I be the only beginner in the room?

Almost never. Our classes mix complete beginners with members who've trained for years, and coaches pair or pad you up appropriately so everyone works at their own level.

What if I'm not very fit yet?

That's exactly what classes are for. Warm-ups and drills are scalable, and coaches will always give you an easier option if you need it — fitness builds with consistency, not before you start.

What should I bring to my first class?

Comfortable training kit, trainers and a water bottle. Gloves, hand wraps and shin guards can usually be borrowed for your first few sessions — no need to buy kit before you know you're staying.

How do I book my free trial class?

Use the "Book Free Trial" button on our membership page, or head to our contact page and a member of the team will get you booked onto the right class.

Classes & Disciplines

Boxing, Muay Thai, K1 & MMA

Is K1 kickboxing the same as Muay Thai?

Not exactly. Traditional Muay Thai includes clinch work, knees and elbows throughout a fight. Our K1 class is Muay Thai-influenced, blending boxing hands, kicks, and some clinch and knee work, coached under a K1 ruleset rather than as a dedicated traditional Muay Thai programme.

Does Fight Hub run a dedicated MMA class?

MMA training runs through our invitation-only Fight Team sessions, where boxing, K1 striking and grappling fundamentals come together, rather than as a separately timetabled beginner class. Our Boxing and K1 classes are the best starting point.

Can I try both boxing and Muay Thai-style kickboxing?

Yes — your free trial can be used on any class, and many members mix boxing and K1 kickboxing once they've joined. There's no requirement to specialise early.

Who coaches at Fight Hub?

Coach Scott Morland is a UCMMA Welterweight Champion (2018) with a background in amateur boxing and professional K-1 kickboxing. Coach Taylor Baker has trained in MMA and boxing, including six months in Thailand, and holds a blue belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Meet the full team on our about page.

Kids & Youth

Training for Younger Members

What age can kids start training?

Kids Boxing runs from age 4-10, Junior Kickboxing from age 9-14, and Youth Team from age 11-15 — all introducing fundamentals in a structured, age-appropriate way.

Do you offer family or sibling discounts?

Get in touch with the team to ask about options for families with multiple children training at Fight Hub.

Can my child try a class before joining?

Yes — the same free trial offer that applies to adult classes covers our Kids Boxing, Junior Kickboxing and Youth Team sessions.

Membership & Pricing

Joining Fight Hub

How much does membership cost?

Adult memberships start from £35/month (Off-Peak Gym, 6am-5pm), with Classes Only at £55/month and Full Gym at £65/month. Kids start from £35/month and Youth Team from £40/month. Prices are current as of our last check — confirm final pricing on the membership page or joining portal.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Most memberships run on a 12-month agreement. Drop-in classes and off-peak gym sessions are available with no commitment at £10 per visit.

Can I just drop in without a membership?

Yes. Drop in for a single class or an off-peak gym session for £10 per visit, either way, with no membership required.
