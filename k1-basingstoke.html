What Is K1?

K1 is a striking ruleset — named after the K-1 kickboxing promotion — that combines boxing's hand speed and defence with the kicks, knees and clinch work of Muay Thai and kickboxing. It's one of the most complete striking disciplines you can train.

New to K1?

Starting K1 in Basingstoke

K1 rewards fighters who are comfortable with hands and feet. Our coaches break every technique down from the fundamentals — jabs and crosses, teeps and roundhouses, clinch entries and knees — so first-timers can train safely alongside more experienced members.

Coach Scott Morland brings real K1 competition pedigree to every session, with a background in professional K-1 kickboxing, amateur boxing, and a UCMMA Welterweight Championship. Progressing members can be invited into Fight Team as their skills develop.

FAQs

K1 in Basingstoke — Your Questions Answered

What is K1?

K1 is a striking ruleset that blends boxing punches with Muay Thai and kickboxing-style kicks and knees, named after the K-1 kickboxing promotion. At Fight Hub, our K1 class trains this combined striking style for fitness, technique and competition.

Who coaches K1 at Fight Hub?

Coach Scott Morland has a background in professional K-1 kickboxing and amateur boxing, and is a UCMMA Welterweight Champion (2018).

Can beginners join K1 classes?

Yes. Classes are coached for mixed-ability groups, with technique broken down from the fundamentals for anyone training K1 for the first time.
