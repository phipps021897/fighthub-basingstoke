New to MMA?

Starting Your MMA Journey in Basingstoke

MMA rewards fighters who are comfortable everywhere — on the feet and in the clinch. We start every member on boxing and K1 to build striking fundamentals, then introduce clinch and grappling concepts as confidence grows.

Progressing members are invited to join Fight Team, our advanced invitation-only session, where coaches with real MMA and BJJ pedigree sharpen technique for competition.

FAQs

MMA in Basingstoke — Your Questions Answered

Does Fight Hub run a dedicated MMA class?

MMA training runs through our invitation-only Fight Team sessions, where boxing, K1 striking and grappling fundamentals come together, rather than as a separately timetabled beginner class. Our Boxing and K1 classes are the best starting point.

Who coaches MMA at Fight Hub?

Coach Scott Morland is a UCMMA Welterweight Champion (2018) with a background in amateur boxing and professional K-1 kickboxing. Coach Taylor Baker has trained in MMA and boxing, including six months in Thailand, and holds a blue belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

Can beginners train MMA at Fight Hub?

Yes. Start with our Boxing or K1 classes to build striking fundamentals — our coaches will guide progressing members towards Fight Team as their skills and experience develop.
