New to Fight Hub?

Your First Class: What to Expect

Starting a new gym can feel daunting — here's exactly what happens before, during and after your first boxing, Muay Thai, K1 or MMA session at Fight Hub.

Book Free Trial View Timetable

Before You Arrive

Aim to arrive 10-15 minutes early for your first visit so you can sign in and have a quick chat with a coach about any injuries or concerns. Wear comfortable training kit you can move freely in, and bring a water bottle.

What to Bring

You don't need to buy any kit before your first session. Gloves, hand wraps and shin guards can usually be borrowed for your first few classes — just bring yourself, trainers and a towel.

What Happens in Class

Every class starts with a warm-up and mobility work, moves into technique broken down step by step, then pad or bag work, and finishes with a conditioning piece. Coaches keep an eye on first-timers throughout.

Nervous About Your First Session?

You Won't Be the Only Beginner in the Room

Every member who trains at Fight Hub now started exactly where you are. Our coaches are used to walking complete beginners through their first class, and sessions are structured so newer and more experienced members can train side by side without anyone feeling out of place.

If you're still unsure which class to start with, our classes page breaks down what each session involves, or you can simply ask when you arrive.

Quick Tips for Your First Class

  • Eat a light meal 1-2 hours beforehand, not a heavy one
  • Let your coach know about any injuries before you start
  • It's fine to pace yourself — work at your own level
  • Most first classes are "watch, try, repeat" — you won't be thrown in blind
  • Stick around after class — most members are happy to chat

FAQs

Your First Class — Common Questions

Do I need any experience to join my first class?

No. Every class is coached for mixed-ability groups, and coaches break technique down from the fundamentals for anyone training for the first time.

Will I be the only beginner in the room?

Almost never. Our classes mix complete beginners with members who've trained for years, and coaches pair or pad you up appropriately so everyone works at their own level.

What if I'm not very fit yet?

That's exactly what classes are for. Warm-ups and drills are scalable, and coaches will always give you an easier option if you need it — fitness builds with consistency, not before you start.

How do I book my free trial class?

Head to our membership page to claim a free trial class, or check the timetable and just turn up 10-15 minutes early to sign in with a coach.

Ready for Your First Class?

Pick a class from the timetable, turn up a little early, and let our coaches take care of the rest.
