New to Combat Sports?

Boxing vs Muay Thai vs K1 vs MMA — What's the Difference?

Four disciplines, four rulesets, one gym. Here's a plain-English breakdown of what separates boxing, Muay Thai, K1 kickboxing and MMA — and how each is trained at Fight Hub.

Boxing

Hands only — jabs, crosses, hooks and uppercuts, built on footwork, head movement and defence. The foundation most striking disciplines are built on.

Hands Only

Muay Thai

Known as "the art of eight limbs" — punches, kicks, knees, elbows and clinch work throughout. Our K1 class draws on Muay Thai's clinch, knees and kicks, run under a K1 ruleset rather than as a separate traditional Muay Thai programme.

8 Limbs

K1 Kickboxing

Boxing hands combined with hard kicks under K1 rules, with clinch and elbows limited or excluded depending on the ruleset variant. A fast, full-contact striking style.

Hands & Kicks

MMA

Combines striking — boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai influence — with clinch and ground grappling, such as wrestling and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. The most complete combat sport.

Striking & Grappling

Which Is Right for You?

Pick a Starting Point, Not a Final Answer

Most members don't choose one discipline for life — they pick a starting point. If you want a fast, technical workout focused on hands, start with Boxing. If you want kicks and clinch work in the mix, K1 is the natural fit.

Want to eventually combine striking with grappling? Boxing and K1 build the base our coaches look for before inviting members into Fight Team and MMA training.

FAQs

Styles Explained — Common Questions

Is K1 kickboxing the same as Muay Thai?

Not exactly. Traditional Muay Thai includes clinch work, knees and elbows throughout a fight. K1 is a kickboxing ruleset that typically allows punches and kicks but limits or excludes clinch and elbows. Our K1 class is Muay Thai-influenced, blending boxing hands, kicks, and some clinch and knee work, coached under a K1 ruleset rather than as a dedicated traditional Muay Thai programme.

Can I train MMA from day one?

You can start working towards it straight away, but we build members up through Boxing and K1 first to establish a striking base, before inviting progressing members into Fight Team, our advanced session where striking, clinch and grappling fundamentals come together.

Which is better for weight loss, boxing or Muay Thai/K1?

Both are demanding full-body workouts and either will support weight loss when combined with consistent training and good nutrition. The right choice usually comes down to which style you enjoy more, since you're more likely to stick with it.

Do I need to pick one discipline, or can I try them all?

You can train across Boxing, K1 and our Youth/Junior programmes — many members do. There's no requirement to specialise early, and trying different classes is a good way to find out what you enjoy most.
