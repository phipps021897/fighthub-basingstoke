New to Boxing?

Your First Boxing Class in Basingstoke

Walking into a boxing gym for the first time can feel daunting — we built Fight Hub to make it easy. You'll be welcomed by a coach, talked through the session, and guided through pad work and drills at a pace that suits you.

There's no pressure to spar until you're ready, and our coaches are happy to adapt sessions around injuries, fitness levels or specific goals, whether that's losing weight, building confidence or working towards your first amateur bout.

FAQs

Boxing in Basingstoke — Your Questions Answered

Do I need boxing experience to join?

No. Our boxing classes welcome complete beginners as well as experienced fighters. Coaches adapt pad work and drills to your level, and sparring is never compulsory.

What age can kids start boxing?

Kids Boxing runs for ages 4–10, introducing boxing fundamentals in a fun, age-appropriate way. Youth Team then runs for ages 11–15.

What should I bring to my first class?

Comfortable training kit, trainers and a water bottle. Hand wraps and gloves can usually be borrowed for your first few sessions.
